QMS Backup - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What this app is

QMS Backup is an internal tool of the QMS Portal, the document control system of our company. It is used by one administrator of the company and is not offered to the public.

What it does with your Google account

After the administrator grants access, the tool writes encrypted backup files of the QMS Portal into one Google Drive folder chosen by the administrator, and reads them back to check them. It does not read, change or delete anything else in the Google Drive account, and it does not use any other Google service.

Data we store and share

The access token is kept only as a secret in the company's private code repository settings. We do not sell, share or send Google user data to anyone else. Backup files are encrypted before they are uploaded, and the encryption password is held only by the company's administrators.

Retention and removal

Backups are kept for the period required by the company's quality system (five years). The administrator can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the backup folder from Google Drive.
